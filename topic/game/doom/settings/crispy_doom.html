Crispy Doom Settings

Last Updated 2025-76-6

My Crispy Doom settings are pretty simple.


~ Go to the first page of the Crispness menu (Options => Crispness),
put High Resolution Rendering to ON, with an Aspect Ratio of 16:9, a Framerate Limit of 210 (Requires Vsync to be OFF and Uncapped Framerate to be ON), and, lastly, Smooth Pixel Scaling set to ON
